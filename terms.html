We provide: IT, Network & Cybersecurity; Website, App & Software; Graphics, Branding & Printing; Logistics & Distribution (including through madukapoa); and commercial Wi-Fi (hotspot). Details of each service are on the Services page.

7. Hosting, Domain na Matengenezo7. Hosting, Domain & Maintenance

Eligible website projects include one year of free hosting, domain and business email, plus one year of maintenance after handover. After that, annual renewal fees apply as listed on the Pricing page. Larger systems with a dedicated VPS or database are quoted separately for renewals.

We will explain the device's condition and the likelihood of success before starting, and we handle your data in confidence as described in the Privacy Policy.

The commercial Wi-Fi service is provided under a written agreement per location, including revenue sharing and the location owner's responsibilities. Platforms such as madukapoa may have their own terms of use.
