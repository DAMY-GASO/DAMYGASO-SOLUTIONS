HADITHI YETU

OUR STORY

Kutoka msaada wa IT hadi mshirika kamili wa teknolojia

From IT support to a full tech partner

Tulianza kwa kutoa msaada wa IT kwa biashara za karibu nasi. Wateja walipoanza kutuuliza pia kuhusu logo, branding, Apps na website, tuliona pengo moja: Biashara nyingi zilikuwa zikitumia watu tofauti kwa kila huduma, jambo linalochelewesha kazi na kupunguza mshikamano wa brand.

We started by offering IT support to businesses near Us. When clients began asking us about logos, branding, Applications and websites too, we noticed a gap: many businesses were juggling different providers for each service — slowing work down and breaking brand consistency.

Leo, Damygaso Tech inatoa huduma zote tatu chini ya paa moja — IT Support & security, Graphic & Brand Design, na Website & App Development — ili wateja wapate ufumbuzi mmoja, thabiti, na wa kuaminika.

Today, Damygaso Tech offers all three under one roof — IT Support & security, Graphic & Brand Design, and Website & App Development — so clients get one consistent, reliable solution.
