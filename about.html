KUHUSU SISI

ABOUT US

Kampuni ya teknolojia ya Kitanzania, yenye maono makubwa A Tanzanian technology company with big ambitions

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited ina makao Dar es Salaam, na ilianzishwa kuziba pengo la kiteknolojia kwa biashara, taasisi za elimu na makampuni ya mikoani kote Tanzania.

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited is headquartered in Dar es Salaam and was established to bridge the technology gap for businesses, educational institutions and regional enterprises across Tanzania.

HADITHI YETU

OUR STORY

Kutoka msaada wa IT hadi mfumo kamili wa kidijitali From IT support to a full digital ecosystem

Tulianza kwa kutoa msaada wa IT kwa biashara za karibu nasi. Wateja walipoanza kutuuliza pia kuhusu logo, branding, apps na website, tuliona pengo moja: biashara nyingi zilikuwa zikitumia watu tofauti kwa kila huduma, jambo linalochelewesha kazi na kupunguza mshikamano wa brand.

We started by offering IT support to businesses near us. When clients began asking about logos, branding, apps and websites too, we noticed a gap: many businesses were juggling different providers for each service — slowing work down and breaking brand consistency.

Leo, DamyGaso Tech Solutions inaendesha mfumo wa kidijitali unaounganisha enterprise IT & cybersecurity, software maalum, miundombinu ya Wi-Fi ya kibiashara, na suluhisho za kidijitali za ugavi wa B2B. Lengo letu: kurahisisha shughuli ngumu ili wateja wetu wakue kwa ufanisi katika uchumi unaozidi kuunganishwa.

Today, DamyGaso Tech Solutions operates an integrated digital ecosystem that combines enterprise IT & cybersecurity, custom software, commercial Wi-Fi infrastructure and digital B2B supply chain solutions. Our aim: simplify complex operations so clients can scale efficiently in an increasingly connected economy.
