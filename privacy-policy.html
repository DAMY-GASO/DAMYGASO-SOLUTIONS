SERA YA FARAGHA

PRIVACY POLICY

Sera ya FaraghaPrivacy Policy

Ilisasishwa mara ya mwisho: Novemba 2026

Last updated: November 2026

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited ("sisi", "yetu") inaheshimu faragha yako. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa tunazokusanya unapotumia tovuti yetu, unapowasiliana nasi au unapotumia huduma zetu, na jinsi tunavyozitumia.

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect when you use our website, contact us or use our services, and how we use it.

1. Taarifa Tunazokusanya1. Information We Collect

Tunakusanya taarifa unazotupatia moja kwa moja unapotumia fomu za "Wasiliana Nasi" au "Weka Order", unapotupigia, au unapotutumia ujumbe wa WhatsApp au barua pepe:

We collect information you give us directly through the "Contact Us" or "Book Now" forms, when you call us, or when you message us on WhatsApp or email:

  • Jina, namba ya simu na barua pepe.
  • Huduma unayoomba na maelezo ya mradi au tatizo lako.
  • Taarifa za biashara au taasisi yako, pale zinapohitajika kwa quote au mkataba.
  • Your name, phone number and email.
  • The service you request and details of your project or problem.
  • Business or organisation details where needed for a quote or contract.

Hatukusanyi taarifa za malipo (kadi au akaunti za benki) kupitia tovuti hii.

We do not collect payment information (cards or bank accounts) through this website.

2. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa2. How We Use Your Information

Tunatumia taarifa zako kujibu ombi lako, kukupa quote, kutekeleza huduma, kukujulisha maendeleo ya mradi, na kuboresha huduma zetu. Hatuuzi wala kukodisha taarifa zako kwa mtu mwingine.

We use your information to respond to your request, provide a quote, deliver the service, keep you updated on your project and improve our services. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

3. Taarifa Tunazozikuta Wakati wa Huduma za IT3. Data We Encounter During IT Services

Wakati wa huduma za IT Support, urejeshaji wa data au matengenezo ya vifaa, tunaweza kufikia faili na mifumo ya mteja. Tunafikia kile kinachohitajika kutatua tatizo tu, hatuzitumii kwa lengo lingine, na hatuzitoi kwa mtu wa nje.

During IT Support, data recovery or equipment repair we may have access to a client's files and systems. We access only what is needed to solve the problem, we do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not disclose it to outsiders.

4. Kazi Zetu na Majina ya Wateja4. Our Work and Client Names

Hatutaji jina la mteja, kampuni yake wala picha za kazi yake kwenye portfolio, tovuti au mitandao ya kijamii bila ridhaa yake. Mteja anaweza kuondoa ridhaa hiyo wakati wowote kwa kuwasiliana nasi.

We do not name a client, their company or show images of their work in our portfolio, website or social media without their consent. A client may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us.

5. WhatsApp na Mawasiliano ya Simu5. WhatsApp & Phone Communication

Ukitumia kitufe cha WhatsApp au kujaza fomu, unaturuhusu kuwasiliana nawe kupitia WhatsApp, simu au barua pepe kuhusu ombi lako. Ujumbe unaotuma kupitia WhatsApp unaongozwa pia na sera za WhatsApp.

By using the WhatsApp button or submitting a form, you allow us to contact you by WhatsApp, phone or email about your request. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also governed by WhatsApp's own policies.

6. Majukwaa na Huduma Nyingine za Kampuni6. Our Other Platforms and Services

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited pia inaendesha majukwaa kama SokoMkononi na madukapoa, pamoja na huduma ya Wi-Fi ya kibiashara. Kila jukwaa linaweza kuwa na sera yake ya faragha inayoeleza taarifa mahususi linazokusanya; sera hii inahusu tovuti hii na mawasiliano yako nasi.

DamyGaso Tech Solutions Limited also operates platforms such as SokoMkononi and madukapoa, as well as a commercial Wi-Fi service. Each platform may have its own privacy policy describing the specific data it collects; this policy covers this website and your communication with us.

7. Usalama wa Taarifa7. Data Security

Tunachukua tahadhari zinazofaa kulinda taarifa zako dhidi ya upotevu, matumizi mabaya au ufikiaji usioruhusiwa. Hata hivyo, hakuna mfumo wa mtandaoni ulio salama kwa asilimia mia moja.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from loss, misuse or unauthorised access. However, no online system is 100% secure.

8. Cookies8. Cookies

Tovuti hii inaweza kutumia cookies za msingi kuboresha uzoefu wako, kama kukumbuka lugha uliyochagua. Hatutumii cookies kwa matangazo ya wahusika wengine. Tovuti inaweza kuonyesha ramani ya Google na viungo vya mitandao ya kijamii, ambavyo vina sera zao.

This website may use basic cookies to improve your experience, such as remembering your chosen language. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising. The site may embed a Google map and links to social networks, which have their own policies.

9. Haki Zako9. Your Rights

Una haki ya kuomba kuona, kurekebisha au kufuta taarifa zako tulizonazo, na kupinga matumizi yake, kwa mujibu wa sheria za Tanzania ikiwemo Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya mwaka 2022. Wasiliana nasi kupitia ukurasa wa mawasiliano au info@damygasotech.com.

You have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of the information we hold about you, and to object to its use, under Tanzanian law including the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. Reach us via the contact page or info@damygasotech.com.

10. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii10. Changes to This Policy

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii mara kwa mara. Toleo jipya litawekwa kwenye ukurasa huu likiwa na tarehe mpya ya usasishaji.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new update date.
