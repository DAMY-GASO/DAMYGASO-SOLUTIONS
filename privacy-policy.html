Privacy Policy

Ilisasishwa mara ya mwisho: Julai 2026

Last updated: July 2026

Damygaso Tech ("sisi", "yetu") tunaheshimu faragha yako. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa gani tunazokusanya unapotumia tovuti yetu au unapowasiliana nasi, na jinsi tunavyozitumia.

Damygaso Tech ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, and how we use it.

1. Taarifa Tunazokusanya

1. Information We Collect

Tunakusanya taarifa unazotupatia moja kwa moja kupitia fomu za "Book Now" au "Wasiliana Nasi", ikijumuisha: jina, namba ya simu, barua pepe, na maelezo ya mradi wako. Hatukusanyi taarifa za malipo kwenye tovuti hii.

We collect information you provide directly through our "Book Now" or "Contact Us" forms, including: your name, phone number, email, and project details. We do not collect payment information on this website.

2. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa

2. How We Use Your Information

Tunatumia taarifa zako kwa: kukujibu ombi lako la huduma, kukupa quote, kukukumbusha kuhusu mradi wako, na kuboresha huduma zetu. Hatuuzi wala kukodisha taarifa zako kwa kampuni nyingine.

We use your information to: respond to your service request, provide a quote, keep you updated on your project, and improve our services. We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp na Mawasiliano ya Simu

3. WhatsApp & Phone Communication

Unapotumia kitufe cha WhatsApp au kujaza fomu, unatuwezesha kuwasiliana nawe kupitia WhatsApp au simu kuhusu ombi lako.

By using the WhatsApp button or submitting a form, you allow us to contact you via WhatsApp or phone regarding your request.

4. Usalama wa Taarifa

4. Data Security

Tunachukua tahadhari zinazofaa kulinda taarifa zako dhidi ya upotevu, matumizi mabaya, au ufikiaji usioruhusiwa. Hata hivyo, hakuna mfumo wa mtandaoni ulio salama kwa asilimia mia moja.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from loss, misuse, or unauthorized access. However, no online system is 100% secure.

5. Cookies

5. Cookies

Tovuti hii inaweza kutumia cookies za msingi kuboresha uzoefu wa mtumiaji. Hatutumii cookies kwa matangazo ya wahusika wengine.

This website may use basic cookies to improve user experience. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

6. Haki Zako

6. Your Rights

Unaweza kuomba kuona, kurekebisha, au kufuta taarifa zako tulizonazo wakati wowote kwa kutuwasiliana kupitia ukurasa wa mawasiliano.

You may request to view, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by reaching us via the contact page.

7. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii

7. Changes to This Policy

Tunaweza kusasisha sera hii mara kwa mara. Mabadiliko yoyote yatawekwa kwenye ukurasa huu na tarehe mpya ya usasishaji.

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new update date.

8. Wasiliana Nasi

8. Contact Us

Kwa maswali kuhusu sera hii ya faragha, wasiliana nasi: +255 743 322 107 au kupitia ukurasa wa mawasiliano.

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us: +255 743 322 107 or via the contact page.
