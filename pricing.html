Maswali kuhusu UhuishajiRenewal FAQs

Kwa nini hosting na domain si bure milele?Why aren't hosting and domain free forever?

Domain na hosting ni huduma zinazotolewa na makampuni mengine ya kimataifa/kitaifa (registrars na server providers), na sisi tunalipia gharama hizo kwa niaba yako mwaka wa kwanza kama sehemu ya kifurushi chako. Gharama hizi zinaendelea kila mwaka bila kujali ni nani anayehudumia tovuti — hakuna kampuni duniani inayoweza kutoa hosting/domain bure milele.

Domain and hosting are services provided by separate international/local companies (registrars and server providers), and we cover that cost on your behalf in year one as part of your package. These costs continue every year regardless of who manages the website — no company anywhere can offer free hosting/domain forever.

Nikikataa kulipa uhuishaji, nini kitatokea?What happens if I don't renew?

Tovuti yako itasimama na kuacha kuonekana mtandaoni (domain ikiisha muda) au itafungwa (hosting ikiisha muda). Lakini Design na code ya tovuti yako bado itakuwa ni mali yako — tunaweza kukuhamishia mahali pengine ukitaka, lakini bila hosting/domain hai, tovuti haitaonekana kwa wateja wako.

Your website will go offline once the domain expires, or be suspended once hosting expires. But the design and code remain yours — we can help transfer them elsewhere if you wish — but without active hosting/domain, the site will not be visible to your customers.

Je, nitaarifiwa kabla ya muda kuisha?Will I be notified before it expires?

Ndiyo, tutakutumia ukumbusho wiki kadhaa kabla ya mwaka wako wa kwanza kuisha, ukiwa na maelezo kamili ya bei na jinsi ya kuendelea, ili usishtukizwe wala tovuti yako isikatike ghafla.

Yes, we'll send you a reminder several weeks before your first year ends, with full pricing details and how to proceed, so there are no surprises and your site never goes down unexpectedly.

Je, bei ya uhuishaji ni ile ile kila mwaka?Does the renewal price stay the same every year?

Kwa kawaida ndiyo, isipokuwa kuna mabadiliko makubwa ya gharama kutoka kwa watoa huduma wa hosting/domain wenyewe. Endapo kutakuwa na mabadiliko, tutakujulisha mapema kabla ya uhuishaji unaofuata.

Typically yes, unless there's a significant cost change from the hosting/domain providers themselves. If that happens, we'll notify you well ahead of your next renewal.
