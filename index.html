Maswali ya Kawaida FAQs

Tovuti ya kampuni ya kawaida huchukua siku 5–10 za kazi, kutegemea na maudhui unayotupatia. E-commerce, marketplaces na apps huchukua muda zaidi.

A standard company website takes about 5–10 working days, depending on how quickly content is provided. E-commerce, marketplaces and apps take longer.

Ndiyo — kila mradi wa website unakuja na mwaka mmoja wa hosting na domain, pamoja na mwaka mmoja wa maintenance bila malipo. Kuanzia mwaka wa pili kuna uhuishaji wa kila mwaka; angalia bei kwenye ukurasa wa Huduma.

Yes — every website project includes one year of hosting and domain, plus one year of free maintenance. From year two an annual renewal applies; see the Services page for rates.

Zote mbili. Huduma za IT support tunaweza kufanya on-site (Dar es Salaam) au remote kwa maeneo mengine ya Tanzania.

Both. IT support can be delivered on-site in Dar es Salaam or remotely for clients elsewhere in Tanzania.

Ndiyo. Kupitia mfumo wetu wa ushirikiano wa 70/30, sisi tunaweka na kusimamia vifaa na mfumo wa malipo, wewe unapata sehemu ya mapato. Wasiliana nasi tuangalie eneo lako.

Yes. Through our 50/50 revenue-sharing model, we install and manage the equipment and billing, and you earn a share of revenue. Contact us and we'll assess your location.

We use a three-part payment plan: 30% to start the project, 40% to complete and 30% to final delivery.
